## Reference (diffusers main, torch 2.15 cu132, `scripts/s2v/oracle.py`)

- 512² bucket: 36736 tokens a forward (4× 384, 2× 512, 1× 2048, ref 1024, noisy 32768).
- bf16 with one block on the card at a time: 19.5-22 s a step, 351 s a 5 s chunk.

- Natural noise of the vendor's bf16 against f32 on the same weights: DiT out 6.8e-2, audio tower 6.8e-2, text 2.2e-2.
