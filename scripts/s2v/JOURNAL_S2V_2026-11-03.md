First pod: RTX 5090, lost to a platform outage; second pod: L40S 46 GB, same image, driver 595.91.

- 5090, q8, 36736 tokens: 16.8 s a step; 616 s for a 7 s clip; final latents 1.8e-2 and 2.5e-2 against the reference, cos 0.9999; VAE 6.8 s a chunk.
- L40S, the torch reference with one block on the card: 19.5 s a step, 312 s a chunk, VAE 10.9 s a chunk.
- L40S, first chain: 21.4 s a step. Class profile of two blocks: flash 315 ms, qkv 80-98, w1 100, w2 90, O 56, speech+prompt scalar 53, row 28, norm 14, dequant 4.
- Prompt attention moved to `qi_flash`: 53 → 10 ms. 256×128 tile on qkv and w2: 90 → 60 and 90 → 72 ms. Two blocks 656 ms.
- Rejected: flash 8×16 (+17 %), 4×32 (+25 %), 8×32 (+15 %), 16×16 (equal); 128×64 GEMM tile (-20 %); polynomial exp2 in the softmax (+8 %); GELU with a quartic exp (no change).
- Device against host on one container: 1.9e-3 (q4tp, 3040 tokens).
- q4tp with the prompt and speech towers at q8: forward 4.4e-2 against f32, q8 2.3e-2; final latents of a chunk 5.1e-2 and 4.1e-2, the vendor's bf16 run being the reference. Frame strips look the same.
- q4tp in the speech tower alone: 78 % off at its last block, so the towers stay q8.

Traps: 20 GB of `write_buffer` without a submit is an OOM; a 2047 MB binding limit forces frame slabs on the VAE's 4.4 M × 256 tensors; `zi_flash` needs a row count that is a multiple of 16.

## Metal, MacBook Air M4 (10-core GPU, 24 GB, no fan)

The Metal path ran on the first try: 3040 tokens against the f32 reference 4.37e-2, the same as q4tp on the device of the pod (4.4e-2).

Baseline on q4tp with one warm file:

- 3040 tokens: 63.6 s a step, 41.1 s of that on the GPU (GEMM 32.4, flash 6.9, norm 1.1).
- 256²: 174-180 s a step (GEMM 96-100, flash 55-58), VAE 94 s a chunk.
- 512², with the first kernel change below: 1260-1363 s a step (GEMM 427-445, flash 823-908), VAE 396 s a chunk.

GEMM is at the limit of the ALUs: 1.57 TF/s in a burst, 1.26 TF/s sustained. A k step of 64 made no difference, 128 lost 24 %, half accumulators lost 42-80 %, a branch-free weight decode with half dequantization gained 3 %. Left as it is.

Two things moved the step:

1. **Views of the mapping.** 20 s of every step were stalls of 2-6 s at a few GEMMs, with the GPU idle. The driver accounts a working set by buffer lengths; two windows over the 24 GB file are 23.4 GB against a limit of 17.8 GB, so it rewired at every switch. One transient view per block (`file_page_buffer`) took 3040 tokens from 60.5 to 39.4 s with the same output, and the peak working set no longer depends on the file.
2. **Self-attention without the running maximum** (`s2v_flash`). `qi_flash` costs twice the GEMM's rate per MAD, and half of the difference is the renormalising branch. The kernel bench on a real panel of 36736 rows, 4 heads:

   | variant | ms | TF/s | rel to exact |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | `qi_flash` | 1751 | 0.79 | 1.95e-4 |
   | lazy maximum, branch at each key block | 1529 | 0.90 | exact |
   | sampled scale, half weights | 934 | 1.48 | 2.4e-4 here, 1.59e-1 over a whole forward |
   | sampled scale, f32 weights (kept) | — | 1.43 on random data | forward 4.372e-2 against 4.369e-2 |

Traps of that kernel, in the order they appeared:

- A bench on random data flatters: scores of real panels run from −10 to over 40, with a mean of 14-28, and fixing the scale at −3 gave 3.8e-1 on the first real forward.
- A check per key block costs as much as the rescaling: 1.30 TF/s without the branch against 0.84 with it, even when it is never taken.
- One real panel flatters too. A scale sampled from 16 key blocks was at 2.4e-4 on block 30, and the forward came out at 1.59e-1, because a few keys in other blocks stand more than 21 bits above the sample. Half cannot hold that range at any scale.
- f32 weights with no scale at all gave 2.88e-1: some blocks pass a score of 100. One sampled scale per query with f32 weights leaves 100 bits above the sample.

Final numbers on q4tp:

- 3040 tokens: 38.1 s a step (flash 4.2 s), forward 4.372e-2 against f32.
- 256²: 134.4 and 148.5 s a step, VAE 98.1 s a chunk, peak footprint 4.68 GB, RSS 7.65 GB.
- 512², full chunk with `--oracle-noise`, under `caffeinate`: 16028 s of wall clock. Steps 1033-1047 s for the first four, then 954-971 s; VAE decode 362.4 s; audio 1.2 s, VAE encode 2.0 s, modulations 2.7 s. Peak footprint 14.65 GB, RSS 8.32 GB. Final latents against the vendor's bf16 run: rel 3.325e-2, cos 0.99945 (L40S: 2.9e-2, 0.9996). Frames look clean, 125 frames with the speech track at 25 fps.
- VAE alone against the reference frames: 397.6 s, 125 frames, rel 8.774e-3, 51.4 dB.
- q8 was not run in full for lack of disk; its GEMM kernel is exercised by the speech and prompt blocks of q4tp.

Rejected on the real panel: skipping the V product when a fragment's weights are negligible (0.56-0.78 TF/s, the branch loses more than the skip wins, with up to 7.7e-2 of error), flushing half denormals (no change), a polynomial exp2 (0.89 of the built-in), 128 or 256 queries per threadgroup (0.82 of 64), staging 64 keys per barrier (0.93), direct device loads (0.95).
