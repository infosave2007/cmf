A detailed watercolour and ink illustration of a red fox curled up asleep in a hollow among the roots of an old oak tree in autumn. Fallen leaves in ochre, rust and deep red are scattered around it, a few acorns and two small brown mushrooms grow beside the roots, and a wren perches on a low twig watching the fox. Loose transparent washes for the background forest, with pale morning light coming through mist between the trunks; fine ink lines for the fox's fur, whiskers and the bark texture. The paper grain is visible, the edges of the painting fade softly into white, and the palette is warm and muted.
