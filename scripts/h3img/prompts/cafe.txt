A cosy street-corner bakery cafe seen from the pavement on a rainy evening, photographed at eye level. Warm light spills from a large window onto wet cobblestones. Painted in gold serif letters on the window glass is the name "MORNING CRUMB", and a small chalkboard on an easel by the door reads "FRESH BREAD 7 AM" in white chalk with a hand-drawn loaf under it. Inside the window, wooden shelves hold round sourdough loaves, croissants and a tray of cinnamon buns; a brass espresso machine gleams on the counter behind them. A red bicycle with a wicker basket leans against the dark green shopfront. Reflections of the window and a street lamp shimmer in shallow puddles. Rich colour, shallow depth of field, fine raindrops visible in the lamp light.
